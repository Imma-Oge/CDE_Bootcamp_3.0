Decision: CI source-data strategy
The CI environment will use its own small representative copy of the raw Facebook Ads and Snapchat Ads source data rather than reading directly from production or using already-transformed development models.

Rationale:
This allows CI to validate the complete dbt transformation path from raw source data through staging, intermediate and final analytical models while maintaining isolation from both development and production. It also prevents CI from requiring direct access to production source data and allows edge cases relevant to automated testing to be represented in a controlled dataset.

📌 Decision log — Raw ingestion architecture
Proposed decision: Ingest the original Facebook Ads and Snapchat Ads datasets into cde_marketing_production.raw, then provision appropriate development and CI raw datasets from that source.
Rationale: Establish one authoritative raw-data location while keeping transformation development and CI testing isolated from production writes.
